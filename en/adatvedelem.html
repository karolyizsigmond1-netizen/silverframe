General information

The data of those requesting a quote through the silverframe.hu website is handled, stored and processed in compliance with the data-protection laws in force at any given time. The relevant rules can be found in this Privacy Policy, which — alongside the data-handling methods — also sets out the measures concerning data protection and the secure handling of personal data.

Our Privacy Policy was prepared with regard to Act VI of 1998 promulgating the Convention on the protection of individuals with regard to the automatic processing of personal data, signed in Strasbourg on 28 January 1981; Act CXII of 2011 on informational self-determination and the freedom of information; and Act CXIX of 1995 on the handling of name and address data for the purposes of research and direct marketing.

Collection, use and handling of personal data

silverframe.hu collects and processes only personal data that the visitor voluntarily provides. We need this data (in particular: name, address, phone number, e-mail address) in order to provide (and improve) the services we offer you, and to achieve the purposes set out in this Privacy Policy.

By providing your data, you expressly consent to its use for the above purposes. In compliance with applicable laws, silverframe.hu stores your personal data only as long as it is justified for the stated purposes, or until you withdraw your consent to data handling.

silverframe.hu will under no circumstances transfer, forward or make your personal data public to any third party other than the company entrusted with data processing, except where required to do so by law, by an authority or by a court.

Protection of personal data

silverframe.hu does everything in its power to handle the data you provide with the highest possible level of security, so that it does not come to the attention of unauthorised third parties.

Modifying, updating and deleting personal data

You may at any time request information about the data silverframe.hu handles about you. Where necessary you may update it, and at any time, without justification or restriction, you may request its modification — and its deletion from the silverframe.hu database — free of charge by writing to info@silverframe.hu.
